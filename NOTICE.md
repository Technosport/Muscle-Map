## 1. Source of Anatomical Data / Source des données anatomiques
The 2D anatomical vector atlas is derived from **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under [CC BY-SA 2.1 JP](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/).

The original 3D models were **modified and adapted** (2D orthographic projections, vector tracing, simplification, custom muscle contours, and color zoning) to produce the Muscle Map standalone atlas.

* **Primary Scientific Reference**:  
  Mitsuhashi N. et al. (2009). *BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts*. **Nucleic Acids Research**, 37(suppl_1), D782–D785.  
  [https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613)

## 2. Usage Rules for Exported Figures / Règles d'utilisation des images exportées
**Allowed** (including academic publications, presentations, commercial use, and private consulting) provided that:

1. **Credit / Attribution**: The visual credit line embedded at the bottom of exported images (*« Muscle Map · Technosport amU · Based on BodyParts3D (DBCLS, CC BY-SA 2.1 JP) »*) satisfies the attribution requirement as long as it remains clearly legible.  
   If the image is cropped or resized without the embedded footer, you must report the citation in the figure caption or notes.
2. **License notice**: Mention the [CC BY-SA 2.1 JP](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/) license.
3. **Indicate modifications**: If you alter the anatomical shapes or vector paths, indicate that changes were made.
4. **ShareAlike (SA)**: Any modified version of the image itself must remain licensed under CC BY-SA. A larger scientific paper, report, slide deck, or book that *contains* the figure is considered a collective/composite work and is **not** required to be open-sourced under CC BY-SA.
